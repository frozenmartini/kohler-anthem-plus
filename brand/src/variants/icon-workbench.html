Home Assistant reads these from custom_components/kohler_anthem_plus/brand/ as icon.png (256×256) and icon@2x.png (512×512), square, and trimmed — the spec forbids padding, borders and transparent spacing around the subject rather than requiring any, so artwork should reach the edges. HACS asks for the same brand/ directory with at least an icon.png in it. The split square behind each hero is the light card meeting the dark card, so an opaque tile sits on the seam unchanged while transparent artwork shows its two very different fates.
