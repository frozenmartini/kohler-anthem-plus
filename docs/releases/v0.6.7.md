**Fixed — 0.6.6 removed the Temperature and Flow numbers instead of renaming them.** Both
entities disappeared from the valve device. This was a straightforward mistake on my part
and not a design change: the edit that dropped a now-unused helper from `ZoneNumberBase`
deleted the rest of that class with it, taking `__init__`, the `_word` accessor and the
slider mode. Both `ZoneTemperatureNumber` and `ZoneFlowNumber` inherit from it, so neither
could be constructed and Home Assistant created neither.

The class body is restored exactly as it was in 0.6.5. `number.py` now differs from that
release by three lines — the import of the shared naming helper and the two names — which is
all 0.6.6 was ever meant to change.

**Your entities come back with the IDs they had.** Unique ids were never touched, so
`number.anthem_valve_zone_1_temperature` and `number.anthem_valve_zone_1_flow` return with
their history and any automation referencing them intact. They will show as `Temperature`
and `Flow`, which is what 0.6.6 was for.

**Checked the rest of that release the same way.** Every file 0.6.6 touched was compared
against 0.6.5 for classes or methods that vanished. `number.py` was the only casualty; the
two removals in `switch.py` are the naming helpers that moved to `entity.py` deliberately,
and `sensor.py`, `entity.py` and `coordinator.py` lost nothing. The renames from 0.6.6 —
`Hex`, and the per-outlet `Rainhead Max Run Time` sensors — are unaffected and stay.

**Upgrading:** update through HACS and restart Home Assistant. The two numbers reappear on
the valve device. If Home Assistant marked them unavailable while they were missing, that
clears on the restart.
