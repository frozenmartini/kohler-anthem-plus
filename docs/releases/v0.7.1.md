**Fixed — 0.7.0 moved the integration but left its icons behind.** The violet mark stopped
appearing in Home Assistant, and the integration fell back to the generic puzzle piece.

Home Assistant's brands proxy serves an integration's icons from *the integration's own*
`brand/` folder. Before 0.7.0 the integration was the repository root, so a root-level
`brand/` was inside it and the icons resolved. The restructure moved everything to
`custom_components/kohler_anthem_plus/` and left `brand/` at the root, which put the PNGs
outside the folder the proxy reads. Nothing in the restructure touched these files, which is
why the review did not catch it — the regression was in what did *not* move.

`icon.png` and `icon@2x.png` now live at `custom_components/kohler_anthem_plus/brand/`. The
icons come back after a Home Assistant restart; they are read at integration load, not on a
browser refresh.

**This also clears the HACS `brands` validation**, which had been failing since the
restructure for the same reason — it looks for exactly
`custom_components/<domain>/brand/icon.png`. Two other HACS checks that were failing are
fixed in the repository's own settings rather than in code: the repository now has topics,
and issues are enabled.

**The design workbench stays out of your install.** `brand/src/` — 39 SVG variants and the
build notes, 224 KB — remains in the repository at the root and is not shipped. Only the two
PNGs the proxy actually reads go out with the integration.

**Upgrading:** update through HACS and **restart Home Assistant**. A browser refresh alone
will not bring the icons back. No entity ids change and no reconfiguration is needed.
