**New action: `probe_usage` — an attempt to find Kohler's water usage history.**

`Total Water Used` is a lifetime counter with an undocumented origin, and the question it
cannot answer is where its total comes from. On the reference account Shower Left reads 8224
gallons while the Konnect app's monthly chart sums to 5613.49 — a gap of about five months at
the observed rate. Pre-charting usage is the likeliest explanation and nothing available
proves it.

**There is an endpoint that would settle it.** `gcs-usage/{deviceId}` answers HTTP **400** to
a bare call while eleven guessed names beside it answer 404 — the route is real and rejected
the call for missing parameters. It is documented nowhere: not in the upstream library, not in
the reference integration, not in any published write-up. This repository's own notes are the
only record it exists, and nothing has ever called it successfully.

The Konnect app shows no lifetime figure, so the app cannot be used to calibrate the counter.
This action is the alternative: it calls that endpoint with fifteen candidate query strings —
date ranges in five spellings, monthly period selectors, year/month, and an epoch range — and
writes the status and shape of each answer to
`custom_components/kohler_anthem_plus/reports/usage_probe_<timestamp>.json`.

**Read-only.** Every call is a GET; nothing on the valve changes. Most candidates are expected
to fail, and the failures are the finding — each one narrows what the parameters can be.

Run it from **Developer Tools → Actions**, pick the valve, and attach the file to an issue.
Skim it first: it carries your device id.

**If it succeeds**, a per-month series would explain the counter's gap directly and could
become a real monthly-usage sensor. **If every candidate fails**, that is worth knowing too —
it would mean the parameters are not guessable and the Konnect APK is the next step.

**Tests: 42 → 44.** Every candidate is checked to survive substitution, since a bad
placeholder would surface as a failed call against live hardware rather than a test failure,
and the probe is asserted to issue no verb but GET.

**Upgrading:** update through HACS and restart Home Assistant. Nothing else changes.
