**Reverted — 0.7.3 divided the water total by four, and it should not have.** Total Water
Used read a quarter of the truth: Shower Left showed 514 gallons where the device reports
2056. This release publishes the counter exactly as the valve sends it, which is what every
release before 0.7.3 did.

**The mistake was mine, and it was a misreading rather than a miscalculation.** The owner
reported the Konnect app showing 2056.00 and 6488.75 for the two valves. Those figures are
exactly the **raw** counter values from the diagnostics captured at 01:59Z that day — so the
app agrees with the raw field and no divisor was ever needed. I matched them instead against
a later capture taken at 11:44Z, where the same counters read 8224 and 25955, and concluded
the device must be counting quarter-gallons.

The 4x between those two captures was the counter advancing during the day. Treating a
change over time as a change of unit is what produced the bug, and the "confirmation to the
cent" was circular: the numbers matched because they were the same numbers, one capture
apart.

**The reference integration was right not to scale this field**, and the earlier analysis
saying so should have carried more weight than a coincidence in two readings.

**On the counter versus the app's monthly chart.** The device counter is a **lifetime**
total and reads higher than the sum of the months the app charts — 8224 against 5613.49 for
Shower Left, a gap of about five months at the reported ~500 gal/month. The most likely
explanation is usage recorded by the valve before the app began charting it: factory test,
commissioning at install, and any period the app has no data for. That is a reading of the
evidence, not something these captures prove; the counter's own origin is not documented
anywhere available.

**History recorded under 0.7.3 through 0.7.5 is a quarter of what it should be.** The
correction reads to Home Assistant as a step up on a `total_increasing` sensor, which it
treats as a meter change rather than a burst of consumption, so no phantom usage is recorded.
The affected span is roughly the last few hours; earlier statistics were never scaled.

**Tests: 43 → 42.** The four tests pinning the divisor are replaced by one asserting the
counter is published unscaled, including both of the owner's app-confirmed readings.
