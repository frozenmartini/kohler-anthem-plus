**The read-only `Max Temperature` and `Max Shower Duration` diagnostics are gone.** 0.18.0 made
both writable; keeping the sensors beside the controls left two entities showing one number,
only one of which could do anything about it.

## What changes on your dashboard

Both diagnostic sensors are removed and their entity registry rows are purged on the next
setup, so nothing is left behind as `unavailable`. The configuration entities added in 0.18.0
report exactly the same values:

| Gone | Replaced by |
|---|---|
| `sensor.…_max_temperature` (diagnostic) | `number` **Max Temperature** — 92-118 °F |
| `sensor.…_max_run_time` (diagnostic) | `select` **Max Shower Duration** — the app's six options |

If either sensor is on a dashboard or in an automation, point it at the control instead. The
control's state is the same figure; for the duration it reads `30 minutes` rather than `30`,
with the number available as `reported_minutes`.

⚠️ **The purge matches on suffix, and that needed care.** The controls' unique ids contain the
same words as the sensors they replace — `_max_temperature_setting` beside
`_max_temperature`. The retired suffixes name the outlet (`_outlet_1_max_temperature`), which
no control id does, so there is no id one careless edit away from deleting the entity the
sensor was retired in favour of. A test asserts that boundary in both directions: the two
sensors purge, and all three controls plus a sample of everything else survive.

## What moved rather than being deleted

**`outlets_agree` and `per_outlet` are now on the duration control.** They are the signal that
a write was lost part-way — the app writes one outlet at a time and stops at the first failure,
leaving the valve holding two different durations. That was established on real hardware in
September, where one valve held 3600 s on its Showerhead and 1800 s on the other two, and
retiring it with the sensor would have thrown away the only way to see that state. Selecting a
duration rewrites every outlet and repairs it.

The tests that covered how the valve's stored tenths are read and converted moved to the
controls too, including the two that document corrections this project had to make: that the
scald limit is a **setting rather than a hardware ceiling** (observed moving from 450 to 477
tenths in seven minutes), and the off-by-one that made the duration read `unknown` while it
knew the answer.

Four tests are gone with the sensors, because they were about those entities rather than about
the valve: their naming, their unique id, and their display units.

## Two documentation gaps closed

0.18.0 added three controls and **documented none of them**. The user guide now describes all
three, their ranges, the verification and its 30-second wait, and the Konnect 3.0.1 defect
around long durations.

It also still described `Max Temperature` as *"Read-only; this integration never writes it"* —
a sentence 0.18.0 had already made false. Both stale rows are gone.

**Tests: 180 → 178.**

**Upgrading:** update through HACS and restart Home Assistant. The two diagnostic sensors
disappear; the controls that replaced them are unaffected.
