**Flow percentage is now a ratio against each outlet's own ceiling, which is what the Konnect
app does.** It was a hardcoded divide-by-two — correct on hardware reporting
`maximumFlowRate: 200`, and wrong anywhere else, where writing "100 %" would have sent
**double the intended flow**.

**This was recorded as unverifiable without differently-configured hardware. That was wrong.**
The app has always been the arbiter and has always been readable. Confirmed from the Konnect
APK's own bytecode:

    jj.h$a.X(value, max)   ->  value * 100 / max     (byte -> percent)
    jj.h$a.Y(percent, max) ->  percent * max / 100   (percent -> byte)

where `max` is `AnthemOutletConfigurationsModel.maximumFlowrate`, read at runtime from the
device's settings. **No divisor of 2 exists anywhere in the app's flow path**, and its slider
takes both bounds from the device — a targeted scan of every flow-touching method found no
`2`, `0.5` or `200` used as a divisor. Even its pre-device placeholder maximum is 100, not
200.

**Nothing changes on hardware reporting 200**, which is every valve in the corpus and both of
the owner's: `byte * 100 / 200` is `byte / 2` exactly, verified across every legal byte
including the odd ones their valves carry. The Flow control still reads 8–100 % in whole
steps and still shows 24 % for byte 49.

**What changed is where the numbers come from.** The slider's bounds, its displayed value and
its writes all now use the ceiling this zone actually reports. `FLOW_PER_PERCENT = 2` survives
only as the default for `decode_word`/`encode_word`, which have 28 call sites and no
per-valve context; there it reproduces the old behaviour exactly.

The outlet's ceiling is published as a `maximum_flow_byte` attribute, because a percentage is
only meaningful beside it: two valves showing "50 %" are at the same fraction of their own
maximum, not necessarily the same flow.

**Tests: 54 → 57**, including an exact-inverse round trip across every legal byte at two
different ceilings, and a regression test pinning the owner's hardware to the display it had
before.

**Upgrading:** update through HACS and restart Home Assistant. No entity ids change, and on a
200-ceiling valve no value changes either.
