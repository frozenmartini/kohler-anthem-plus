**New: `Water Used This Month` — the figure the Konnect app charts.** Kohler's usage-history
endpoint now backs a real sensor, on every valve, carrying the whole monthly series as a
`history` attribute so a year-on-year comparison needs no second call.

The endpoint is one no other integration calls. Its query parameters turned out to be
**PascalCase** (`FromDate`, `ToDate`, `Interval`) where the rest of the API is camelCase,
which is why it answered the same generic 400 to fifteen reasonable guesses and to a bare
call alike. Solved in 0.7.8 from the Konnect app's own Retrofit annotations; live-verified
here.

**`volume` is in litres.** Not gallons, and not the account's unit — litres always. The app
multiplies by `0.264172` when `waterUnits` is `Standard`, and this follows that exactly, so
the sensor matches the app rather than merely coming close. August 2026 on the reference
valve reads **478.4 gal**, against the owner's recollection of "about 500 gallons a month".

Getting that wrong was easy and nearly happened: summed across fourteen months the raw
figures come to 14,137, which **exceeds** the valve's lifetime `totalFlow` of 8,224 — an
impossibility that was the clue. Converted, the window is 3,735 gal, which fits under the
lifetime total, and the implied rate falls from 4.89 to 1.29 gal/min. A shower runs at
roughly 1.5–2.5.

**And it answers the standing question about the lifetime counter.** `Total Water Used` and
this sensor measure the same water in different units from different sources, which is why
they never reconciled by inspection.

**Three fields are deliberately not surfaced.** `averageBlendTemperature` and
`numberOfTimesValveSwitchedOn` have **no call sites anywhere in the Konnect app** — it parses
and ignores them — and the temperatures observed (~78, against real setpoints of 104.9 °F)
match no plausible unit. The `avg*` rollups are per-**day**, not per-period: `avgVolume` is
the range total over the inclusive day count, making `avgAverageBlendTemperature: 2.55` a
per-day average of a monthly temperature. All of it is recorded in `docs/gcs/api.md` and
none of it is published as a state.

**`TOTAL`, not `TOTAL_INCREASING`.** The figure resets each month by design, and telling Home
Assistant otherwise would make every month boundary look like a meter replacement.

**Read once at setup**, beside the configuration. A monthly total moves slowly and Kohler's
own chart is not live either, so it refreshes on restart or reload rather than costing a call
on every reconnect.

**`probe_usage` stays**, now only for seeing raw responses; the sensor is the answer for
day-to-day use.

**Tests: 44 → 48.** The conversion is pinned to a real captured response, both unit settings
are covered, the current month is asserted to be matched by key rather than by position — the
series can end on an empty month — and a failed read is asserted to read `unknown` rather
than a stale number.

**Upgrading:** update through HACS and restart Home Assistant. The new sensor appears on each
valve device.
